How CampusCARE Works

A transparent 3-step loop between students and maintenance teams

01
📝 Snap & Report

Select room/block, pick problem category, add a description, and attach an optional photo proof.

02
🔄 Dispatch & Work

Relevant campus cell accepts the ticket, dispatches technicians, and logs real-time updates.

03
✅ Resolve & Rate

Staff marks issue resolved with resolution remarks. Student verifies fix and provides a star rating.
